Math priorities

Math priorities

Estimate: 1 hour≈ 2 min read

Prerequisites: Math formulas

Here is the order of priority for studying mathematics to become an Applied ML/AI Engineer.

1. Linear Algebra (high priority)

The foundation for representing and operating on data.

Topics:

  • Scalar, vector, matrix, tensor.
  • Matrix multiplication.
  • Dot product.
  • Transpose.
  • Matrix inverse.
  • Eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
  • Norm and vector similarity.

2. Calculus (high priority)

The foundation of how neural networks are trained.

Topics:

  • Functions.
  • Limits.
  • Derivatives.
  • Partial derivatives.
  • Chain rule.
  • Gradient.
  • Gradient descent.

3. Probability & Statistics (high priority)

The foundation of data analysis and prediction.

Topics:

  • Mean, median, mode.
  • Variance and standard deviation.
  • Probability.
  • Conditional probability.
  • Bayes theorem.
  • Normal distribution.
  • Sampling.
  • Hypothesis testing.
  • Correlation and covariance.

4. Optimization (important)

Topics:

  • Gradient descent.
  • SGD.
  • Momentum.
  • Adam.
  • Learning rate.
  • Convex and non-convex optimization.

5. Discrete Mathematics (supporting)

Topics:

  • Set theory.
  • Logic.
  • Combinatorics.
  • Graph theory.
  • Basic combinatorial probability.

Summary of the mathematics: if time is limited, focus on:

  1. Matrix and vector operations.
  2. Derivatives and the chain rule.
  3. Probability and distributions.
  4. Statistics and hypothesis testing.
  5. Gradient descent and optimization.

You do not need to become a pure mathematician. The goal is to be able to read, understand, and implement mathematical concepts in ML models.
